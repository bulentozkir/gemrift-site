Controls

Looking around

Hold the Look around key (V on the keyboard, the left shoulder button on a controller). The clock stops, Milo stays where he is, and the arrow keys, WASD or the stick move the view so you can see the rest of the cave. Let go and the view returns to Milo.

Mini map: Ctrl+Z shows it, Ctrl+X hides it, or click its X button.
Game

The clock

Time is measured in simulation ticks. By default Strict clock is off: reaching zero removes the remaining-time bonus but play continues. With Strict clock on, zero ends the attempt, as in classic Gemrift, unless a surviving player completes the exit on that same tick.

Fresh attempt. Unlimited retries. Press [R] to Dig Again.

Mini map

A small aerial photo of the whole cave sits in the top-right corner of the cave view. The mini map shows the real cave as seen from above, with its dirt, rocks, gems, creatures and water, and it changes as the cave does. The white box marks the part of the cave you can see, and the bright dot with a ring is Milo. When Milo walks behind the map, it turns see-through. It is shown in every cave until you close it: press Ctrl+X, or click the X button on its corner. Press Ctrl+Z to show it again. It stays shown or hidden the way you left it, in the next caves too.

Need a little light? Solution recordings

Levels 1-5 recordings are free for everyone. From level 6 onward, the bulb stays visible; an active full-game Microsoft Store license and two failed attempts on the same cave unlock recordings for every cave, including ones already cleared. After 9 failed attempts on one cave, that cave's recording also unlocks for everyone, on Windows and Mac, with or without the full game. Failures are saved per cave across sessions and difficulties; manual restarts, quitting, and skipped caves do not count, though a gate retry can count as another failed attempt. Click the bulb to watch only that cave's downloaded, verified MP4. It loops automatically; Replay starts it again, and Close returns to the paused game. On a Mac the recording opens in your video player instead, and the game waits until you click Close. Missing recordings and failed license checks are reported.

Armored in a spiked dark shell with a pulsing ember core. It hugs the left wall of any passage and detonates in a fiery 3×3 blast on contact with Milo or a falling rock.
