UNDERGROUND BESTIARY / CAVE CREATURES & HAZARDS

Friends to outsmart. Foes to turn into treasure.

Every creature in Gemrift follows deterministic rules you can read at a glance—and many reward a well-timed boulder drop with a shower of bonus gems.

LEFT-WALL PATROL

Cinder Beetle (Firefly)

Armored in a spiked dark shell with a pulsing ember core. It hugs the left wall of any passage and detonates in a fiery 3×3 blast on contact with Milo or a falling rock.

⚡ Explosive hazard • Keep your distance
GEM BEARER

Prism Moth (Butterfly)

Flutters along the right-hand wall on broad, gem-faceted violet and turquoise wings. Crush a Prism Moth beneath a falling boulder to crystallize the blast into collectible gems!

💎 Drops a 3×3 cluster of gems when crushed
CAVE ALCHEMY

Living Amoeba & Magic Wall

Green amoeba colonies slowly spread through open tiles—hem them in with boulders so they suffocate and crystallize into gems! Or drop stones through purple Magic Walls to transmute rock into treasure.

🧪 Enclose amoebas to transform them into gems
ABYSSAL BIOME

Lantern Eel & Prism Ray

In flooded subterranean caverns, sinuous blue Lantern Eels sweep the dark with bioluminescent lures while diamond-winged Prism Rays glide by carrying pink dorsal gems.

🌊 Aquatic wall-followers in underwater caves
TRENCH HUNTERS

Deep Octopus & Reef Shark

Coral-hued Octopuses curl eight tapering arms along left-hand cavern walls, while streamlined blue Reef Sharks patrol right-hand currents and burst into gems when crushed.

🦈 Crush Reef Sharks with boulders for bonus gems
GLACIAL PATROL

Polar Bear & Snow Tiger

Frost-cavern guardians with distinct patrol axes: heavy Polar Bears pace horizontally back and forth across icy corridors, while striped Snow Tigers prowl vertical shafts and drop gems when defeated.
