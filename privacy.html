LEGAL / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026 • Publisher: Bulent Ozkir • App ID: 9P3QHCNQ5SL3

Gemrift ("we", "our", or "us") is a Windows puzzle-action cave adventure developed and published by Bulent Ozkir. We respect your privacy and designed both this website and the Gemrift desktop game so you can play without surrendering personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • No Personal Data Collection: Neither the Gemrift game nor this website collects, sells, rents, or transmits personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location).
  • No Accounts or Tracking SDKs: Gemrift does not require user registration, login credentials, third-party analytics trackers, or advertising cookies.

2. Local Game Data on Your Device

Gemrift stores your game settings, unlocked caves, difficulty selections, and high scores locally on your Windows PC under your user profile (%APPDATA%\Gemrift\save.json). When you watch built-in cave solution recordings ("Show me resolution"), the game streams the MP4 walkthrough over HTTPS and caches it locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Gemrift\ RecordingsCache so subsequent views play offline. You can delete these local files at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the folders.

3. Microsoft Store Trial, Licensing & Installation

Gemrift is distributed via the Microsoft Store (https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p3qhcnq5sl3?hl=en-US&gl=US) with a 1-day free trial and a paid full version unlocking the complete 215-cave campaign and all solution videos. Billing, trial status verification, and license management are handled directly by Microsoft through the Windows Store APIs. Microsoft's own Privacy Statement governs transactions and Store telemetry.

4. Website Hosting

This website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages. We do not set tracking cookies or run analytics scripts. Standard web server logs (such as IP addresses requested for security and content delivery) are processed by GitHub in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Children's Privacy

Gemrift is a family-friendly cave puzzle adventure featuring Milo Coppercap. Because the game and website do not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Gemrift GitHub repository.
