2. Game Edition & Licensing

Gemrift is available on Windows 10 and Windows 11 via the Microsoft Store. Players receive a 7-day free trial to explore the underground caverns, after which the paid full version unlocks permanent access to all 215 cave maps across 5 difficulty tiers (Junior, Senior, Principal, Expert, Distinguished) and the complete library of built-in solution walkthroughs.
